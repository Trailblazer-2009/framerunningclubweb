Welcome to One Step Beyond

We’re a friendly and inclusive frame running club based in the UK. Whether you’re completely new to frame running or already an experienced athlete, we provide a supportive space for people of all abilities to build fitness, confidence and friendships.

About Our Club

Our Frame Running Club is a fun and friendly place where everyone is welcome! We love helping people discover the joy of frame running — it’s all about freedom, confidence, and having a great time together. Whether you’re here to make new friends, get active, or chase your goals, we’ll be cheering you on every step of the way!

About Our Mission

One Step Beyond was founded to give people with physical disabilities an opportunity to experience the freedom of movement and competition through frame running. We meet regularly for training sessions and take part in events across the UK.

We believe sport should be fun, inclusive and empowering. Our volunteer coaches and helpers are passionate about creating a welcoming atmosphere where everyone can achieve their personal goals.

Join Us

We welcome children, teenagers and adults of all abilities. You don’t need any experience — just an interest in trying frame running! We have frames available for new members to try at sessions.

How to Join:

  • Come along to one of our monthly sessions.
  • Try a frame and meet our coaches.
  • We’ll help you find the right frame size and get started.

Contact us at one.stepbeyond@outlook.com to book your first session.
